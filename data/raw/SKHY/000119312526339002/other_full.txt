Relationship between the Company or its largest shareholder and each counterparty to the disposal

 

 

•

Reason for selection of counterparties to the disposal

 

 

•

To implement compensation for independent directors currently in office as of the payment date, reflecting
changes to compensation in consideration of the expansion of legal liability of directors, the increasing sophistication of their roles and the broadening scope of business areas subject to board oversight, as well as competitive compensation levels
and the level of difficulty of their duties.

•

Basis for price calculation
