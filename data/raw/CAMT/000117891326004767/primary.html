FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

 

CAMTEK PRE-ANNOUNCES RECORD Q3 REVENUES

ABOVE GUIDANCE; PROVIDES RECORD Q4 GUIDANCE

AND INITIAL REVENUE OUTLOOK FOR 2027

 

Q3 revenues expected to be $160-161 million; 2026 revenue guidance at

approximately $595 million and 2027 initial revenue outlook at over $800 million

 

MIGDAL HAEMEK, Israel – October 14, 2026 – Camtek Ltd (NASDAQ: CAMT; TASE: CAMT), in advance of its upcoming investor and analyst breakfast, announced today that its revenue for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, is expected to be slightly above the previously issued guidance range and also introduced its revenue guidance for full year 2026 and an initial outlook for year 2027.

 

Camtek expects revenue for the third quarter of 2026 to be $160-161 million, representing a year-over-year increase of 27%.

 

The company also provided its initial guidance for the fourth quarter of 2026 of approximately $180 million, up 40% year-over-year, which will bring the full year 2026 revenues to approximately $595 million.

 

Since the beginning of the year, the Company has received orders of over $800 million, with deliveries scheduled throughout 2026 and into 2027. Given this strong order momentum and visibility, Camtek management provided its initial revenue outlook for 2027 at over $800 million, representing estimated growth of more than 35% over 2026.

 

The third quarter revenue figures are preliminary and are subject to the completion of the Company’s quarter-end closing procedures. Full financial results for the third quarter of 2026 will be reported with the Company’s quarterly results announcement expected in early November.

 

Rafi Amit, Camtek’s CEO commented, “I’m excited about the all-time record revenue levels. The exceptional order intake we have experienced this year, driven by the very strong demand for advanced packaging for AI applications, has enabled us to build a strong backlog and pipeline, giving us visibility for 2027. We expect the growth will continue to be driven by our next-generation products, led by the Hawk and the Eagle G5, as we ramp quickly with customers who continue to expand capacity to meet market demand. We look forward to discussing our growth strategy and our updated longer-term financial model with our investors and analysts at our upcoming investor and analyst breakfast event in San Francisco.”

 

Camtek’s management will discuss this outlook, together with the Company’s growth strategy, technology roadmap and updated longer-term financial model, at its Investor & Analyst Breakfast Presentation at SEMICON West today, October 14, 2026.

ABOUT CAMTEK LTD.

 

Camtek is a developer and manufacturer of high-end inspection and metrology equipment for the semiconductor industry. Camtek’s systems inspect IC and measure IC features on wafers throughout the production process of semiconductor devices, covering the front and mid-end and up to the beginning of assembly (Post Dicing). Camtek’s systems inspect wafers for the most demanding semiconductor market segments, including Advanced Interconnect Packaging, Heterogenous Integration, Memory and HBM, CMOS Image Sensors, Compound Semiconductors, MEMS, and RF, serving numerous industries’ leading global IDMs, OSATs, and foundries.

 

With manufacturing facilities in Israel and Germany, and eight offices around the world, Camtek provides state of the art solutions in line with customers’ requirements.
