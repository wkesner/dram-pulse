Notes Offering

On August 10, 2026, Silicon Motion Technology Corporation, a Cayman Islands exempted company (the “Company”), issued a press release
announcing that it intends to offer, subject to market and other conditions, $800,000,000 aggregate principal amount of convertible senior notes due 2031 (the “Notes”) in a private offering to persons reasonably believed to be
“qualified institutional buyers” pursuant to Rule 144A under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”) (the “Offering”). The Company also expects to grant the initial purchasers of the Notes an
option to purchase, for settlement within a period of 13 days from, and including, the date the Notes are first issued, up to an additional $120,000,000 aggregate principal amount of Notes. A copy of the press release announcing the Offering is
furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Report on Form
6-K
 (this “Report”).
