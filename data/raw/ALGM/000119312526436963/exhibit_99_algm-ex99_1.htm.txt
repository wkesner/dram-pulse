Allegro MicroSystems Reports Second Quarter 2027 Results
Second Quarter Sales Increased by 29% Year-over-Year to $277 Million
Record Industrial & Other and Data Center Sales
GAAP EPS Quadrupled and Non-GAAP EPS Doubled Year-over-Year
 

Manchester, NH, October 29, 2026 –
 Allegro MicroSystems, Inc. (“Allegro” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: ALGM
), a global leader in power and sensing semiconductor solutions for motion control and energy efficient systems, today announced financial results for its second quarter ended September 25, 2026.
 
“We saw continued strong momentum in our fiscal second quarter, delivering our seventh consecutive quarter of sequential sales growth. Sales were $277 million, representing a 29% increase year-over-year. GAAP earnings per share quadrupled from $0.03 per share in the fiscal second quarter of 2026 to $0.12. Non-GAAP EPS grew to $0.26, doubling over the second quarter of fiscal 2026. Broad year-over-year sales growth in Automotive and Industrial end markets drove this strong performance, led by data center, which established a new record,” said Mike Doogue, President and CEO of Allegro. “Our forward demand signals strengthened significantly this quarter. We delivered our highest quarterly design-win performance since our 2020 IPO—up nearly 40% year-over-year—and bookings increased for the eighth consecutive quarter. With record design wins, a robust backlog, and expanding content across our target Automotive and Industrial markets, the leading indicators across our business give us high confidence in our path to exceed our target model of mid-teens annual sales growth.”
 
“During the quarter, we also continued to execute on our strategic priorities of expanding profitability and generating strong cash flow,” said Derek D’Antilio, Executive Vice President and CFO of Allegro. “On September 30, 2026, following the close of the second quarter, we made another $25 million voluntary debt repayment, underscoring the strength of our balance sheet and our confidence in Allegro’s anticipated free cash flow generation.”

Business Outlook
For the third quarter of fiscal year 2027 ending December 25, 2026, the Company expects total net sales to be in the range of
$278 million to $288 million. At the midpoint of this range, it implies growth in net sales of 23% year-over-year.
The Company also estimates the following results on a non-GAAP basis:
•
Gross Margin is expected to be between 51.5% and 52.5%,
•
Operating expenses are expected to be $87 million, plus or minus $1 million, and
•
Diluted Earnings per Share is expected to be between $0.25 and $0.29, with the mid-point of this range implying an 80% year-over-year increase.
 
 
Allegro has not provided a reconciliation of its third fiscal quarter outlook for non-GAAP Gross Margin, non-GAAP Operating Expenses, and non-GAAP Diluted Earnings per Share because estimates of all of the reconciling items cannot be provided without unreasonable efforts. It is difficult to reasonably provide a forward-looking estimate between such forward-looking non-GAAP measures and the comparable forward-looking U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”) measures. Certain factors that are materially significant to Allegro’s ability to estimate these items are out of its control and/or cannot be reasonably predicted.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
In addition to the measures presented in our condensed consolidated financial statements, we regularly review other measures, defined as non-GAAP financial measures by the SEC, to evaluate our business, measure our performance, identify trends, prepare financial forecasts and make strategic decisions. The key measures we consider are non-GAAP Gross Profit, non-GAAP Gross Margin, non-GAAP Operating Expenses, non-GAAP Operating Income, non-GAAP Operating Margin, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA margin, non-GAAP Profit before Tax, non-GAAP Income Tax Provision, non-GAAP Effective Tax Rate, non-GAAP Net Income Attributable to Allegro MicroSystems, Inc., non-GAAP Basic and Diluted Earnings per Share, non-GAAP Free Cash Flow, and non-GAAP Free Cash Flow as a percentage of net sales (collectively, the “Non-GAAP Financial Measures”). These Non-GAAP Financial Measures provide supplemental information regarding our operating performance on a non-GAAP basis that excludes certain gains, losses and charges of a non-cash nature or that occur relatively infrequently and/or that management considers to be unrelated to our core operations, and in the case of non-GAAP Income Tax Provision, management believes that this non-GAAP measure of income taxes provides it with the ability to evaluate the non-GAAP Income Tax Provision across different reporting periods on a consistent basis, independent of special items and discrete items, which may vary in size and frequency. These Non-GAAP Financial Measures are used by both management and our board of directors, together with the comparable GAAP information, in evaluating our current performance and planning our future business activities.
The Non-GAAP Financial Measures are supplemental measures of our performance that are neither required by, nor presented in accordance with, GAAP. These Non-GAAP Financial Measures should not be considered as substitutes for GAAP financial measures, such as gross profit, gross margin, net income or any other performance measures derived in accordance with GAAP. Also, in the future we may incur expenses or charges, such as those being adjusted in the calculation of these Non-GAAP Financial Measures. Our presentation of these Non-GAAP Financial Measures should not be construed as an inference that future results will be unaffected by unusual or nonrecurring items. These Non-GAAP Financial Measures exclude costs related to acquisition and related integration expenses, amortization of acquired intangible assets, stock-based compensation, restructuring actions, related-party activities and other non-operational costs.
Non-GAAP Income Tax Provision
In calculating the non-GAAP Income Tax Provision, we adjust for the tax effect of adjustments to GAAP results which represents the estimated income tax effect of the adjustments to non-GAAP Profit before Tax described below. We also adjust for any discrete tax items and the impact of non-recurring tax law changes to ensure the non-GAAP Effective Tax Rate (“NG ETR”) reflects future operations.
Our fiscal year 2026 and 2027 NG ETR excludes the impact of the 2025 One Big Beautiful Bill Act’s one-time research and development amortization election which accelerates the amortization of previously capitalized domestic research and development over a two-year period. The NG ETR is applied to non-GAAP Profit before Tax to arrive at the tax effect of adjustments to GAAP results.
