Changes in Nokia Corporation's own
shares

 

Espoo, Finland – A total
of 126 758 Nokia shares (NOKIA) held by the company were transferred today without consideration to participants of Nokia's equity-based
incentive plans in accordance with the rules of the plans. The transfer is based on the resolution of the Board of Directors to issue
shares held by the company to settle its commitments to participants of the incentive plans as announced on 22 November 2024.

 

The number of own shares held
by Nokia Corporation following the transfer is 149 267 180.
