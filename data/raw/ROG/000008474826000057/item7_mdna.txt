Company Overview
We design, develop, manufacture, and sell high-performance, high-reliability engineered materials and components that help customers solve complex challenges in connectivity, thermal management, power electronics, system protection, and other areas. We operate in two reportable segments: AES and EMS. Our remaining non-core operations are reported in our Other operating segment. We are headquartered in Chandler, Arizona.
Our strategy is to build a high-growth, high-performing company by strengthening our positions in attractive core markets and extending proven technologies and manufacturing capabilities into compelling adjacent opportunities.
Our core end markets include industrial, automotive, aerospace and defense, electronics, and communications. Demand in these markets is supported by market trends including aerospace and defense investment, electrification, higher levels of vehicle safety, factory automation, supply chain regionalization, and increasing demand for premium electronic devices.
Our targeted high-growth adjacent opportunities include AI data centers, advanced battery systems, and electromagnetic interference shielding, where we are leveraging established products, applications expertise, manufacturing capabilities, and customer relationships to address evolving performance requirements.
Our sales, applications engineering and technical service teams work closely with customers throughout the development process to understand system-level challenges and develop customized solutions. This customer-focused model, together with our broad product portfolio, global manufacturing footprint, regional technical support, and reputation for mission-critical reliability, supports strong customer relationships and our growth plans.
We are operating with greater focus and accountability to make faster decisions, shorten development cycles, and respond more quickly to customer needs. Our operational improvement initiatives include improving quality, yields, and throughput; strengthening procurement and supplier strategies; advancing continuous improvement; and maintaining a scalable cost structure.
We intend to continue investing in innovation, technical capabilities, and manufacturing capacity in a disciplined manner, while preserving financial flexibility and allocating capital to opportunities that we believe offer attractive risk-adjusted returns and create long-term shareholder value.
If we successfully execute this strategy, we believe we can increase annual revenues through a combination of growth in our core markets and targeted high-growth adjacent opportunities. We expect organic growth to be the primary driver, with the potential to be supplemented by disciplined acquisitions that add capabilities, strengthen market positions, or accelerate our strategic priorities. Achieving this revenue growth is also expected to result in higher profitability and earnings.

Restrictions on Payment of Dividends
The Fifth Amended Credit Agreement generally permits us to pay cash dividends to our shareholders, provided that (i) no default or event of default has occurred and is continuing or would result from the dividend payment and (ii) our total net leverage ratio does not exceed 2.75 to 1.00. If our total net leverage ratio exceeds 2.75 to 1.00, we may nonetheless make up to $20.0 million in restricted payments, including cash dividends, during the fiscal year, provided that no default or event of default has occurred and is continuing or would result from the payments. Our total net leverage ratio did not exceed 2.75 to 1.00 as of September 30, 2026.
