BENCHMARK DELIVERS RECORD THIRD QUARTER REVENUE AND STRONG EARNINGS GROWTH
RAISES FISCAL YEAR 2026 OUTLOOK
 
 
TEMPE, AZ, October 28, 2026 – Benchmark Electronics, Inc. (NYSE: BHE) today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
 
Third quarter 2026 results:
•
Revenue of $811 million, up 19% year-over-year
•
Diluted GAAP earnings per share of $0.47, up 21% year-over-year
•
Diluted non-GAAP earnings per share of $0.84, up 35% year-over-year
•
Operating cash flow of $30 million with free cash flow of $14 million
"Our third quarter results reflect the continued transformation of Benchmark into a higher-performing company," said David Moezidis, Benchmark's President and CEO.
Moezidis continued, "Demand is broadening across our portfolio, bookings momentum remains healthy, and our teams continue to execute at a high level. As a result, we delivered record quarterly revenue, grew earnings at nearly twice the rate of sales, and are once again raising our outlook for the year. We now expect 2026 revenue growth of approximately 16%, further increasing our outlook beyond the $3 billion annual revenue level we established last quarter."

About Benchmark Electronics, Inc.
Benchmark provides comprehensive solutions across the entire product lifecycle by leading through its innovative technology and engineering design services, leveraging its optimized global supply chain, and delivering world-class manufacturing services in the following industries: advanced computing and communications, aerospace and defense, industrial, medical, and semiconductor capital equipment. Benchmark’s global operations include facilities in eight countries and its common shares trade on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol BHE.
