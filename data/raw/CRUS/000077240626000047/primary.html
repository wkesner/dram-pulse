Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure

Cirrus Logic, Inc. (the “Company”) is aware of certain reports stating that it recently made efforts to acquire Synaptics Incorporated (“Synaptics”) and is the party referred to as “Party A” in filings made with the Securities and Exchange Commission by Synaptics and ON Semiconductor Corporation (“onsemi”) in connection with their pending merger. The Company confirms that it made acquisition proposals and is Party A. However, the Company does not otherwise adopt or confirm the accuracy of the descriptions of the Company or its proposals contained in the referenced filings.

The Company is not currently pursuing a transaction with Synaptics and will not be providing any further comment on this matter at this time.

The information in this Item 7.01 is being furnished and shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section, nor shall it be deemed incorporated by reference in any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
