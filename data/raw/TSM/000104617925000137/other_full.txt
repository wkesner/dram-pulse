TSMC November 2025 Revenue Report
HSINCHU, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Dec. 10, 2025 -
 TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenue for November 2025: On a consolidated basis, revenue for November 2025 was approximately NT$343.61 billion, a decrease of 6.5 percent from October 2025 and an increase of 24.5 percent from November 2024. Revenue for January through November 2025 totaled NT$3,474.05 billion, an increase of 32.8 percent compared to the same period in 2024.
