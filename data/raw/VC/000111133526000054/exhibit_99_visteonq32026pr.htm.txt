Visteon Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
VAN BUREN TOWNSHIP, Mich.,
October 22, 2026
— Visteon Corporation (NASDAQ: VC) today reported third quarter financial results.

Highlights include:

Sales of $970 million with Growth-over-Market of 10%

Third Quarter Results
Visteon reported net sales of $970 million, compared to $917 million in the prior year, an increase of 6% year-over-year. Despite customer vehicle production declining by 5%, the Company delivered 10% growth-over-market
1
, driven by strong launch execution and robust customer demand.
Net income attributable to Visteon was $5 million or $0.19 per diluted share. Gross margin in the third quarter was $47 million and includes $77 million of exceptional warranty charges. Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure defined below, was $46 million, reflecting underlying continued operational execution in a challenging supply chain environment.
For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, cash from operations was $81 million, capital expenditures were $103 million, and adjusted free cash flow, a non-GAAP measure defined below, was an outflow of $4 million. The Company ended the quarter with cash of $466 million and debt of $330 million. The Company's strong balance sheet, with a net cash position of $136 million, provides flexibility to continue investing in the business while returning capital to shareholders.
Visteon secured $0.9 billion in new business during the third quarter, reflecting continued momentum across display products and in India, a key growth market. Highlights included two driver display awards with a luxury Japanese OEM brand for vehicles in Japan and China, a center display for multiple vehicles with a high-volume Indian OEM, a display and infotainment system for multiple vehicle models with another Indian OEM, and a center display for the commercial vehicle market in Europe with a North American OEM. The quarter also included a number of strategic awards with North American OEMs and for the Asian two-wheeler market.

Visteon launched 21 new products during the third quarter across 14 customers. Most notably, the Company launched the industry-first High-Performance Compute system, with integrated AI capabilities, for the Chery Freelander 8 in the Chinese market. Other highlights included a follow-on launch of our fast-growing Audi Panorama program on the Audi A2 and A3, a cockpit domain controller for the BMW i3 in Europe, a two-wheeler digital cluster for the Royal Enfield Interceptor in India, and a driver display for the all-new Lexus TZ. These launches demonstrate demand for our next-generation products while providing the foundation for future sales performance.
“Our third quarter results highlight continued progress toward sustainable sales growth while delivering strong profitability in a challenging supply chain environment,” said President and CEO Sachin Lawande. “Our HPC launch with Chery was a significant milestone for Visteon, demonstrating our ongoing position as a key supplier to support the transition to software and AI-defined vehicles.”
Accelerated Share Repurchase Program
In the third quarter, the Company announced and completed a $200 million accelerated share repurchase ("ASR"), delivering on the Company's commitment to returning capital to shareholders while maintaining the flexibility to invest in future growth in a dynamic environment.
Revised Guidance
Based on year-to-date performance and our outlook for the fourth quarter, Visteon is raising its full-year 2026 sales guidance range to $3.80 – $3.85 billion, while lowering its full-year adjusted EBITDA range to $370 – $410 million, and its adjusted free cash flow range to $90 – $130 million. The adjusted EBITDA range reflects the impact of $77 million of exceptional warranty charges incurred in the quarter as well as cost pressures, while the adjusted free cash flow range takes into account year-to-date inventory build.
About Visteon
Visteon (NASDAQ: VC) is advancing mobility through innovative technology solutions that enable a software-defined future. The Company's state-of-the-art product portfolio merges digital cockpit innovations, advanced displays, AI-enhanced software solutions, and integrated EV architecture solutions. With expertise spanning passenger vehicles, commercial transportation, and two-wheelers, Visteon partners with global OEMs to create safer, cleaner, and more connected journeys. Headquartered in Van Buren Township, Michigan, Visteon operates in 17 countries, with a global network of innovation centers and manufacturing facilities. For more information, visit visteon.com.
