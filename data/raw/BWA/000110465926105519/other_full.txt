Item 7.01
Regulation FD Disclosure

 

In response to questions it has received, BorgWarner Inc.
(the “Company”) reiterates its prior statements that the Company continues to expect turbine generator production to begin
in Hendersonville, North Carolina in 2027, with an initial 2 GW of installed capacity.  At present, the Company continues to expect
turbine generator sales to be more than $300 million during the first year of production.
