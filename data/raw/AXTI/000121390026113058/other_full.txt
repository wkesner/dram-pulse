Introductory Note

 

References to “AXT”
refer to AXT, Inc. and references to “Tongmei” refer to Beijing Tongmei Xtal Technology Co., Ltd. For purposes of this report,
amounts in Renminbi, or RMB, have been translated into approximate amounts in U.S. dollars solely for the convenience of the reader.

 

Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive
Agreement

 

On October 27, 2026, Tongmei and AXT, as Tongmei’s controlling shareholder, entered into a Share Subscription Agreement, a Supplemental
Agreement (Investor’s Rights Agreement) and a Redemption Support Agreement (collectively, the “Agreements”) with BNK-B3
HK Holdings Limited, a limited liability company duly established and validly existing under the laws of Hong Kong, which entity is managed
by a leading Asia asset manager (the “Lead Investor”), and certain institutional investors, including an institutional investor
who is an affiliate of an existing customer (collectively, the “Investors”). The Agreements provide for an investment by the
Investors of a minimum of RMB 1,912,350,000 (approximately US$285 million) (the “Minimum Amount”) and up to a maximum of RMB
3,668,639,053.25 (approximately US$545 million) (the “Maximum Amount”) in the ordinary shares of Tongmei, subject to customary
closing conditions. Concurrently with the Agreements, AXT agreed to recapitalize Tongmei by subscribing for 342,998,797 ordinary shares
for the RMB equivalent of US$400 million on or prior to the closing of transactions contemplated by the Agreements. Tongmei intends to
use the proceeds for further expansion of its manufacturing capacity for its indium phosphide semiconductor substrates, expansion of its
principal business operations, capital expenditures and general working capital. Assuming the Maximum Amount of the investment, AXT will
own approximately 73.40% of Tongmei following the consummation of the transaction.

 

The investment in Tongmei by the Investors will occur in one or more closings. Under the Supplemental Agreement (Investor’s Rights
Agreement), Tongmei, AXT and the Investors undertake to use their reasonable best efforts to achieve a Qualified IPO (as defined below)
of Tongmei as soon as possible after the closing. The closing of the Minimum Amount is subject to a number of customary conditions, including,
but not limited to, receipt of required filings from applicable Governmental Authorities (as defined in the Agreements). Additional institutional
investors consisting of strategic partners designated by Tongmei and Tongmei-approved designees of the Lead Investor, may participate
in the offering within the Maximum Amount on the same price and terms, provided that the closing of the purchase of shares by such designated
investors is completed by December 31, 2026. If closing(s) for the Minimum Amount by the Investors have not occurred by December 20,
2026, the Share Subscription Agreement will terminate, although the parties agreed to reasonably consider an extension if required approvals
by Governmental Authorities, as defined in the Agreements are still pending. Subject to the termination provisions of the Share Subscription
Agreement, the Lead Investor may also terminate the Share Subscription Agreement before closing if Tongmei or AXT materially breaches
any of the Agreements and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) business days, or if their representations are not true in all
material respects. No assurance can be given that the Investors will invest the Maximum Amount, or that any closing will occur.

 

Investor Rights.
 Under
the Supplemental Agreement (Investor’s Rights Agreement), the Lead Investor will have the right to nominate one (1) director (the
“Investor Director”) to Tongmei’s nine (9) member board, and AXT will nominate the other eight (8) directors. The Lead
Investor will have the right to consent to certain customary transactions that may have an adverse effect on the Investors’ investment
as provided for in such agreement and will have certain information and inspection rights so long as the Investors collectively hold more
than 4.5% of Tongmei’s ordinary shares. The Investors will also have certain preemptive rights if Tongmei proposes to issue additional
securities, which exclude securities that may be issued in connection with a Qualified IPO, pursuant to an equity incentive plan, or in
a transaction whose primary purpose is not fundraising as approved by the board (including the Investor Director). The Investors will
also have a liquidation preference on a sale or liquidation of Tongmei and priority on Tongmei profit distributions.

Redemption Rights.

Under the Supplemental Agreement (Investor’s Rights Agreement), each Investor has the right, but not the obligation, to require
Tongmei to redeem its shares if (i) Tongmei has not completed a Qualified IPO by the three (3)-year anniversary of the closing date, (ii)
any of the following occurs and has resulted in, or would reasonably be expected to result in, a material adverse effect that is not cured
within fifteen (15) business days: (A) a material breach by Tongmei or AXT of a transaction document, (B) a material violation of applicable
law by Tongmei (or certain of its subsidiaries) or AXT (or any of its subsidiaries), or (C) a final conviction of a criminal offense by
(x) any officer, director or employee of Tongmei arising out of the operations and/or business of Tongmei, or (y) Tongmei, any of its
subsidiaries or AXT in connection with their respective operations and/or business; (iii) Tongmei becomes bound, without the Lead Investor’s
consent, by redemption obligations to other holders at more than US$2.3360 per share or exceeding US$50 million in the aggregate; or (iv)
a change of control of Tongmei or of AXT occurs (in the case of AXT, only if AXT common stock ceases to be registered under Section 12(b)
of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”)), other than a permitted drag-along or co-sale transaction.
The term “Qualified IPO” means the first underwritten public offering of Tongmei’s equity securities on a qualified
exchange, including the Main Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, with an offering price per share that would enable each Investor to
achieve a return equal to or exceeding an annual internal rate of return of thirteen percent (13%), compounded annually. In the event
of a notice of a redemption right by an Investor, the per share redemption price equals (i) the per share subscription price paid by the
Investor; (ii) a return on the per share subscription price, calculated at the per annum rate equal to eight percent (8%) (the “Applicable
Rate”), compounded annually on each anniversary of the closing date for the period from the closing date to the exit date, plus
(iii) any declared or accrued but unpaid dividends or distributions through the exit date.

Purchase Option
.
Under the Redemption Support Agreement, upon an Investor’s exercise of its redemption right pursuant to a redemption notice, AXT
may, but is not obligated to, elect to purchase the shares covered by the notice at the redemption price instead of Tongmei. AXT may
pay in cash (which AXT may satisfy with an unsecured promissory note), in shares of AXT common stock valued at the volume-weighted average
price on Nasdaq over the ninety (90) consecutive trading days ending the trading day before the exit date (only if the agreed conditions
are satisfied), or a combination of cash (including the promissory note) and AXT common stock (only if the agreed conditions are satisfied),
with the same form of payment applying to all exercising Investors. Any promissory note will be unsecured, will have a principal amount
equal to the cash portion of the purchase price, and will bear interest at a per annum rate equal to the Applicable Rate. AXT must prepay,
nine (9) months after issuance, the lesser of the note principal amount and 100% of the Investor’s original subscription amount,
with the balance due one (1) year after the exit date, extendable at AXT’s option for one (1) additional year. Under the Redemption
Support Agreement, the Investors have a co-sale right on transfers of Tongmei equity securities by AXT, and AXT has a drag-along right
to require the Investors to join in a sale of all of Tongmei’s equity securities to a non-affiliate for cash or freely tradable
listed securities if the Investors receive at least the greater of 1.5 times their investment or a 13% annual internal rate of return.
