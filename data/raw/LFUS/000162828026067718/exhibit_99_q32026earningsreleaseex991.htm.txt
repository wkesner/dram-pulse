Littelfuse Reports Third Quarter Results for 2026
Third Quarter Highlights:
(Year-over-year comparisons unless otherwise noted)
•
Record net sales of $767 million, including a reduction of $28.5 million related to a one-time Dortmund contract amendment fee recorded as a reduction of semiconductor net sales
•
Organic growth contributed +22%
•
Cash flow from operations of $230 million, +57%; Free cash flow of $216 million, +64%
•
YTD cash flow from operations of $457 million, +55%; YTD free cash flow of $409 million, +66%
•
GAAP diluted earnings per share of $4.49, +62%
•
Record adjusted diluted earnings per share of $5.79, +96%
•
GAAP operating margin of 20.0%, +440 bps; Adjusted EBITDA margin of 28.4%, +690 bps
CHICAGO, October 28, 2026  - Littelfuse, Inc. (NASDAQ: LFUS), a leader in developing smart solutions that enable safe and efficient electrical energy transfer, today reported financial results for its third quarter ended September 26, 2026:
“We delivered strong third quarter results reflecting broad-based demand strength, disciplined execution, and
continued momentum across the portfolio,” said Greg Henderson, Littelfuse President and Chief Executive Officer.
“Our teams drove significant growth across our segments, while delivering meaningful margin expansion and robust
cash generation as we continued to advance our strategic priorities.”
“Looking ahead to the fourth quarter, we continue to see strong demand across our diverse market exposures, and
we expect approximately +28% yr/yr revenue growth. We remain focused on scaling our high growth opportunities,
partnering with our market leading customers, enhancing operational excellence, and deploying capital with
discipline as we execute our long-term strategy.”
Fourth Quarter of 2026*
Based on current market conditions, for the fourth quarter the Company expects:
•
Net sales in the range of $750 - $770 million, adjusted diluted EPS in the range of $5.05 - $5.25 and an adjusted effective tax rate of approximately ~24%

Third Quarter 2026 Segment Performance Highlights
Electronics Segment
•
Net sales for the third quarter 2026 increased to $426 million, including the previously disclosed $28.5
million (€24.6 million) one-time Dortmund contract amendment fee recorded as a reduction of
semiconductor net sales. Organic sales increased +27% driven by improved passive products (+30%
organic). Semiconductor products (+24% organic) also contributed to growth driven by increased protection
and power semiconductor volumes.
•
Adjusted EBITDA margin for the third quarter 2026 increased to 33.1% (+910 bps) due to volume leverage,
operational execution, and favorable price and product mix.
Transportation Segment
•
Net sales for the third quarter 2026 increased +7%, both reported and organic, to $183 million. Organic
sales growth benefited from improved commercial vehicle sales (+20% organic), which offset lower
passenger vehicle organic sales (-5%). Commercial vehicle sales growth benefited from improved truck,
data center, material handling, construction and agricultural equipment demand. Lower passenger vehicle
sales were impacted by lower global passenger car builds and auto sensor product declines.
•
Adjusted EBITDA margin for the third quarter 2026 increased to 19.6% (+280 bps) driven by commercial
vehicle volume leverage and operational execution.
Industrial Segment
•
Net sales for the third quarter 2026 increased +65% to $158 million. Organic sales increased +27% driven
by improved data center, grid & utility infrastructure, HVAC, industrial automation, and construction
demand. The Basler Electric Company (“Basler”) acquisition contributed +38% to growth.
•
Adjusted EBITDA margin for the third quarter 2026 increased to 25.2% (+450 bps) driven by improved
volume leverage, operational efficiencies, and favorable price and product mix.
Dividend
•
The Company announced a quarterly cash dividend of $0.80 per share on its common stock. The dividend will be paid on December 3, 2026, to shareholders of record as of November 19, 2026.

About Littelfuse
Littelfuse, Inc. (NASDAQ: LFUS) is a diversified, industrial technology manufacturing company empowering a sustainable, connected, and safer world. Across more than 20 countries, and with approximately 18,000 global associates, we partner with customers to design and deliver innovative, reliable solutions. Serving over 100,000 end customers, our products are found in a variety of industrial, transportation and electronics end markets – everywhere, every day. Learn more at
Littelfuse.com
