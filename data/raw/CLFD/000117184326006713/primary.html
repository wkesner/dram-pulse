Item 5.02. Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

 

On October 15, 2026, Clearfield, Inc. (the “Company”) and Cheryl Beranek, President and Chief Executive Officer of the Company, entered into a transition agreement (the “Transition Agreement”), to facilitate Ms. Beranek’s retirement on March 31, 2027 and the smooth transition to a successor President and Chief Executive Officer. The Transition Agreement was recommended by the Compensation Committee (the “Compensation Committee”) of the Board of Directors of the Company (the “Board”) and approved by the Board, and amends in part the Employment Agreement dated December 16, 2008 (the “Employment Agreement”) between the Company and Ms. Beranek.

 

Pursuant to the Transition Agreement, Ms. Beranek will continue in her role as President and Chief Executive Officer until the earlier of March 31, 2027 or the Company’s appointment of a successor President and Chief Executive Officer. Upon the appointment of a successor President and Chief Executive Officer, Ms. Beranek will remain employed by the Company through March 31, 2027 (the “Transition Period”) as a non-executive officer with the honorific title of President and Chief Executive Officer Emeritus to provide transition and other assistance and, at the request of the Company, will resign from any other officer roles and as a director of the Company.

 

During the Transition Period, Ms. Beranek’s annual base salary of $483,000 and participation in employee benefit plans and programs will remain unchanged, except that Ms. Beranek will not be granted any future equity awards. The Compensation Committee has not yet adopted the fiscal year 2027 annual cash performance bonus opportunity (the “2027 Incentive Bonus”) under the Company’s Incentive Bonus Plan. The Transition Agreement provides that Ms. Beranek will participate in the 2027 Incentive Bonus, with a bonus opportunity equal to 100% of her base salary. For the purposes of the Employment Agreement, Ms. Beranek’s termination of employment at March 31, 2027 will be treated as a termination without Cause, subject to Ms. Beranek execution of a general release in favor of the Company that becomes effective. Ms. Beranek will be entitled to the amounts and benefits under the Employment Agreement in connection with a termination without Cause except that the Transition Agreement provides that the pro-rated annual bonus to which Ms. Beranek would be entitled in respect of the 2027 Incentive Bonus will not be prorated and will be an amount equal to 100% of her base salary.

 

The foregoing summary does not purport to be complete and is subject to, and qualified in its entirety by, reference to the full text of the Transition Agreement, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated by reference herein.
